# Blood pressure classification

Blood pressure should be classified using the average of two or more careful readings taken on two or more occasions. Normal blood pressure is a systolic pressure below 120 mmHg and a diastolic pressure below 80 mmHg. Elevated blood pressure is a systolic pressure of 120 to 129 mmHg with a diastolic pressure below 80 mmHg.

Stage 1 hypertension is a systolic pressure of 130 to 139 mmHg or a diastolic pressure of 80 to 89 mmHg. Stage 2 hypertension is a systolic pressure of at least 140 mmHg or a diastolic pressure of at least 90 mmHg. When systolic and diastolic readings fall into different categories, the higher category is used.

# Treatment

Non-drug measures are recommended for all adults with elevated blood pressure or hypertension. These include weight loss, a heart-healthy diet, reduced sodium intake, increased potassium intake, regular physical activity and reduced alcohol intake.

First-line drug classes for hypertension are thiazide diuretics, calcium channel blockers, and angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors or angiotensin receptor blockers. Blood pressure should be monitored after starting or changing treatment until the target is reached.
